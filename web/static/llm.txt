## Commission Terms & Guidelines
- **Payment**: FULL PAYMENT MUST BE DONE FIRST after the approval of the commission.
- **Refund Policy**: If the artist fails to provide the commission requested because of circumstance, the client will be REFUNDED.
- **AI & NFTs**: DO NOT use artwork to train AI or for NFTs.
- **Credit**: If sharing or posting the commission, the artist must be credited properly.
- **Copyright & Commercial Use**: The artwork provided is the artist's intellectual property. Commercial, business, & project purposes must be discussed thoroughly through DMs.
- **Progress**: Weekly updates of progress are sent to the client via DMs of their preferred messaging platform.
- **Rates**: Determined by the scope and complexity of the requested artwork. Clients are invited to direct message for tailored quotes. Rush deliveries may incur an increased rate.
