## Commission Terms & Guidelines
- **Rates**: Determined by the scope and complexity of the requested artwork. Clients are invited to direct message for tailored quotes.
- **Progress**: Weekly progress updates are provided to commissioners throughout the workflow.
- **Deadlines**: Rush deliveries may incur an increased rate.
- **Usage**: Personal use only (avatars, banners, personal wallpapers, private prints) unless a commercial license has been explicitly purchased.
- **Copyright**: The artist retains copyright and promotional rights to display created artwork in portfolios and art books.
- **Payment**: Work commences once the agreed upfront deposit (50% minimum) or full payment is received.
